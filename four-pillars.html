PILLAR 01 • SHARIRIK, MANASIK, BAUDDHIK & ADHYATMIK VIKAS

Integrated Development

Core Essence • What It Means

Educating the whole child in complete balance — physical vitality, emotional poise, sharp intellect, and inner spiritual strength.

Daily Practice on Campus:
Body • Sharirik

Morning Surya Namaskar & Yoga

Daily pranayama and stretching drills for physical stamina and agility.

Intellect • Bauddhik

Hands-on STEM Lab Experiments

Direct testing in physics, chemistry, biology, and computer labs.

Vitality • Khel-Kood

Inter-House Campus Athletics

Year-round football, cricket, volleyball, and track training.

Diagnostic • Manthan

ASSET Diagnostic Assessments

Skill analytics (Std III–IX) to personalize each student’s growth.

FOCUS: Physical Vitality Advanced STEM Labs Athletics & Sports ASSET Analytics
PILLAR 02 • BHARTIYA SANSKRITI EVAM SANSKAR

Indian Culture & Heritage

Core Essence • What It Means

Rooting students in timeless Indian traditions, Vedic wisdom, and deep lifelong respect for family, teachers, and society.

Daily Practice on Campus:
Reverence • Parampara

Daily Guru Paduka Pooja

Assemblies open with lamp lighting, Paduka Pooja, and Vedic chants.

Scripture • Swadhyaya

Bhagavad Gita Recitation

Chanting competitions fostering Sanskrit diction and ethical depth.

Values • Balvihar

Balvihar Value Education

Dedicated periods exploring character ethics, epics, and family values.

Celebration • Utsav

Vedic Cultural Festivals

Grand celebrations for Guru Purnima, Janmashtami, and Navratri.

FOCUS: Guru Paduka Pooja Bhagavad Gita Recitation Balvihar Values Vedic Festivals

Student-led community awareness rallies and rainwater conservation.

Civic Action • Swachhata

Boisar Cleanliness Drives

Hands-on environmental sanitation, tree planting, and civic outreach.

Inspiration • Veergatha

Tributes to National Heroes

Commemorations honoring armed forces, scientists, and martyrs.

FOCUS: National Anthem Jal Pakhwada Drive Cleanliness Campaigns Hero Commemorations

Universal Outlook

Guiding students to embrace the Upanishadic truth that the entire cosmos is one interconnected family living in harmony.

Youth Model Assemblies
