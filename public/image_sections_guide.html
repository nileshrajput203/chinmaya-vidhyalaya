Section-Wise Image Directory

All active website photos are now structured cleanly inside public/images/sections/ folder. You can easily locate and replace any image section by section.

How to Replace Any Image:
Navigate to public/images/sections/<section_folder>/ on your computer, paste your new photo, and rename it to the exact file name shown below. The website will instantly reflect the updated image!
